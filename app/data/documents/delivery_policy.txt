SYNAPSE-AI DELIVERY OPERATIONS POLICY

1. Delivery Performance

Orders should normally be delivered within the committed delivery window.

A delivery is considered late when the actual delivery time exceeds the committed delivery time.

2. Late Order Escalation

If the late-order rate exceeds 5% of total orders for a reporting period, the Operations Manager must investigate the underlying cause.

If the late-order rate exceeds 10%, the issue must be escalated to the Regional Operations Head.

3. Operational Investigation

When late deliveries increase significantly, the investigation should consider:

- Delivery capacity
- Driver availability
- Route planning
- Geographic demand concentration
- Order volume
- Weather or external disruptions
- Warehouse or fulfillment delays

4. Corrective Actions

Possible corrective actions include:

- Reallocating delivery capacity
- Adjusting delivery schedules
- Reviewing high-delay delivery zones
- Increasing driver capacity during peak periods
- Reviewing warehouse processing time
- Improving route planning

5. Management Review

Management should review delivery performance together with:

- Revenue trends
- Order volume
- Operating costs
- Customer activity

A significant deterioration across multiple indicators should trigger a broader operational review.

6. Documentation

All major operational issues and corrective actions should be documented.

The responsible manager should record:

- Problem identified
- Root cause
- Corrective action
- Responsible team
- Expected resolution date
- Follow-up status
